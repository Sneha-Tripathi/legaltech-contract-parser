How does LegalTech work?
Upload your legal contract in PDF format. Our AI extracts the text, detects legal clauses, identifies risks, and generates a comprehensive analysis report — all within seconds.
Is my contract secure?
Absolutely. All uploaded contracts are stored securely and are only accessible to the authenticated user who uploaded them. Your data remains private and confidential.
What file formats are supported?
Currently we support PDF format. We are working on adding support for DOCX, TXT and other popular document formats in future updates.
How is risk calculated?
Our AI analyzes each detected clause against a set of predefined risk indicators. Each clause is evaluated and assigned a risk level — High, Medium, or Low — based on the severity and nature of the legal language used.
Can I download reports?
Yes. Every analyzed contract can be downloaded as a professional PDF report. The report includes the contract summary, detected clauses, risk analysis, and key findings.
How long does analysis take?
Most contracts are analyzed within seconds. The processing time depends on the size and complexity of the document, but typically completes in under 30 seconds.
Can I upload multiple contracts?
Yes. You can upload and analyze as many contracts as you need. All your contracts are stored in your personal dashboard and can be accessed anytime.
Is login required?
Yes. You need to create an account and log in to upload contracts and view analysis reports. This ensures your documents are stored securely and privately.
Can I print reports?
Absolutely. Each contract analysis page includes a print button that lets you print the complete report directly from your browser.
